One-Pot Chicken Parmesan Pasta
A one-pot weeknight pasta with bite-sized chicken, a jar of marinara, and penne cooked right in the sauce, finished with melty mozzarella. Kid-friendly, no wine or olives. Adapted from Well Plated (5 stars, 21 reviews).

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 20 minutes
Total Time: 30 minutes

INGREDIENTS
• 1 tablespoon olive oil
• 1 pound boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into bite-size pieces
• 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
• 0.25 teaspoon garlic powder
• 0.25 teaspoon salt
• 0.25 teaspoon black pepper
• 2 cloves garlic, minced
• 1 jar (24 ounces) marinara sauce
• 1.5 cups water
• 8 ounces penne pasta
• 1.5 cups shredded mozzarella
• 0.25 cup grated Parmesan
• 2 tablespoons fresh basil or parsley, chopped

STEPS
1. Heat the olive oil in a large deep skillet or pot over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and sprinkle with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Cook about 4 minutes until golden and cooked through, then remove to a plate.
2. Add the minced garlic to the pot and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Pour in the marinara and water. Bring to a strong, steady simmer, then stir in the penne. Cover.
4. Cook 11-15 minutes, stirring several times so the pasta doesn't stick, until the penne is al dente.
5. Stir the chicken back in. Add the mozzarella a handful at a time, stirring between additions, then stir in the Parmesan. Taste and adjust seasoning.
6. Garnish with basil or parsley and serve.

NOTES
• Uses pantry staples: a jar of marinara and penne pasta.
• To stretch it further, use the full 16 ounces of pasta and add an extra splash of water.
• Stir in a few handfuls of baby spinach at step 5 for greens.
