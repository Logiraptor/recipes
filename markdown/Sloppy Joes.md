Sloppy Joes
Ground beef simmered in a sweet-tangy ketchup and tomato paste sauce, piled on buttered toasted hamburger buns. Straight out of a 90s weeknight dinner. Adapted from Spend With Pennies (4.91 stars, 100 votes) — https://www.spendwithpennies.com/easy-sloppy-joes/

Servings: 5
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 25 minutes
Total Time: 35 minutes

INGREDIENTS
• 1 pound lean ground beef
• 0.5 yellow onion, finely diced
• 0.5 teaspoon garlic powder
• 0.5 green bell pepper, finely diced
• 0.67 cup water
• 0.5 cup tomato sauce
• 0.25 cup ketchup
• 3 tablespoons tomato paste
• 2 tablespoons brown sugar
• 1.5 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
• 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
• 1 teaspoon yellow mustard
• 0.5 teaspoon salt
• 0.25 teaspoon black pepper
• 5 hamburger buns
• 2 tablespoons butter, for the buns

STEPS
1. Cook the ground beef, onion, and garlic powder in a 10-inch skillet over medium-high heat, breaking up the meat, until no pink remains. Drain off excess fat.
2. Add the bell pepper, water, tomato sauce, ketchup, tomato paste, brown sugar, vinegar, Worcestershire, mustard, salt, and pepper. Stir and bring to a boil.
3. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered about 15 minutes, until thickened and scoopable.
4. Meanwhile, butter the cut sides of the buns and toast them under the broiler or in a dry skillet 2 to 3 minutes until lightly browned.
5. Spoon about a half cup of the meat mixture onto each bun and serve.

NOTES
• Uses the same ground beef, onion, and tomato paste as the chili, and the same green bell pepper as the Cajun chicken and rice.
• Add a slice of American cheese on top of the meat before closing the bun.
• Serve with potato chips or oven fries and pickle spears.
