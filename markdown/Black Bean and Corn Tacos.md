Black Bean and Corn Tacos
Quick vegetarian tacos built on a can of black beans and a can of corn, with a creamy cumin-lime slaw and avocado. Kid-friendly and pantry-driven. Adapted from Cookie and Kate (5 stars, 88 reviews).

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Total Time: 25 minutes

INGREDIENTS
• 1 tablespoon olive oil
• 1 small yellow onion, chopped
• 2 cloves garlic, minced
• 1 can (15 ounces) black beans, drained and rinsed
• 1 can (15 ounces) corn, drained
• 1 tablespoon ground cumin
• 0.5 teaspoon chili powder
• 0.33 cup water
• 0.25 teaspoon salt
• 0.25 cup mayonnaise
• 1 lime, juiced (about 1 tablespoon)
• 0.5 teaspoon ground cumin
• 0.25 teaspoon salt
• 0.5 teaspoon granulated sugar
• 4 cups coleslaw mix (shredded cabbage and carrots)
• 2 green onions, sliced
• 8 small corn tortillas
• 1 avocado, sliced
• 1 jalapeno, sliced, optional

STEPS
1. Make the slaw dressing: stir together the mayonnaise, lime juice, 0.5 teaspoon cumin, 0.25 teaspoon salt, and sugar.
2. Combine the coleslaw mix and green onions in a bowl, pour the dressing over, and stir to coat. Refrigerate until serving.
3. Warm the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook 5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add the black beans, 1 tablespoon cumin, and chili powder; stir 30 seconds. Pour in the water, cover, and simmer 5 minutes.
5. Uncover and mash about half the beans with a fork. Stir in the corn and heat through, 2-3 minutes. Season with the 0.25 teaspoon salt.
6. Toast the tortillas in a dry skillet over medium heat until lightly browned on each side. Stack and cover with foil to keep warm.
7. Build each taco with the bean-corn filling, a scoop of slaw, avocado slices, and jalapeno if using. Serve warm.

NOTES
• Uses pantry staples: 1 can black beans and 1 can corn.
• Reuses the same cumin-lime slaw as the Fish Tacos recipe — swap in fish later if you want.
• Add crumbled feta or shredded cheese on top, or a spoonful of salsa, for extra flavor.
