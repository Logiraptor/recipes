Classic Beef and Bean Chili
The plain, mild, one-pot chili everybody grew up on: ground beef, two cans of beans, diced tomatoes, and tomato paste. Serve with shredded cheddar and crackers. Adapted from Budget Bytes (4.82 stars, 230 votes) — https://www.budgetbytes.com/basic-chili/

Servings: 6
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 35 minutes
Total Time: 45 minutes

INGREDIENTS
• 2 tablespoons olive oil
• 1 yellow onion, diced
• 2 cloves garlic, minced
• 1 pound ground beef (80 to 85 percent lean)
• 1 can (15 ounces) kidney beans, drained
• 1 can (15 ounces) black beans, drained
• 1 can (15 ounces) diced tomatoes, with juices
• 1 can (6 ounces) tomato paste
• 1 cup water
• 1 tablespoon chili powder
• 1 teaspoon ground cumin
• 0.25 teaspoon cayenne pepper
• 0.25 teaspoon garlic powder
• 0.5 teaspoon onion powder
• 0.5 tablespoon brown sugar
• 1 teaspoon salt
• 0.5 teaspoon black pepper
• 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar, for serving
• 0.5 cup sour cream, for serving, optional

STEPS
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion and garlic and cook until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the ground beef and cook, breaking it up, until fully browned. Drain off excess fat if needed.
3. Stir in the kidney beans, black beans, diced tomatoes with their juices, tomato paste, and water.
4. Add the chili powder, cumin, cayenne, garlic powder, onion powder, brown sugar, salt, and pepper. Stir until the tomato paste is fully incorporated.
5. Cover and simmer over low heat at least 30 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick.
6. Taste and adjust salt. Serve hot topped with shredded cheddar and sour cream, with crackers or tortilla chips.

NOTES
• Mild as written — skip the cayenne entirely for the toddler's bowl and let adults add hot sauce at the table.
• Uses the same black beans, cumin, and chili powder as the black bean tacos, and the same tomato paste and cheddar as the one-pot mac.
• Even better the next day; leftovers make good chili dogs or nacho topping.
