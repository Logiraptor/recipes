Simple Pan Broccolini
Stupid-simple pan-seared broccolini with garlic — one skillet, five minutes of prep, tender-crisp and lightly charred. Mild enough for kids.

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Total Time: 15 minutes

INGREDIENTS
• 1 pound broccolini, trimmed
• 2 tablespoons olive oil
• 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
• 0.25 teaspoon salt
• 0.25 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
• 2 tablespoons water

STEPS
1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the broccolini and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 4-5 minutes until bright green and starting to char.
3. Add the garlic (and red pepper flakes, if using) and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add the water, cover, and steam 2-3 minutes until the stems are tender-crisp.
5. Uncover and cook off any remaining liquid. Serve immediately.

NOTES
Great as a side for fish, chicken, or rice bowls. Squeeze fresh lemon over the top if you like.
