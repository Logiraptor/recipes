Coconut Curry with Air-Fried Cod
A from-scratch coconut curry built on an onion-tomato-ginger-garlic masala, made in the Instant Pot and finished with full-fat coconut milk and lime. Served over basmati rice and topped with crispy air-fried cod instead of chicken. Dairy-free and gluten-free. Adapted from pipingpotcurry.com (4.91 stars, 585 votes).

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 25 minutes
Total Time: 40 minutes

INGREDIENTS
For the coconut curry sauce:
• 1 tablespoon ghee or oil
• 1 teaspoon cumin seeds, optional
• 1 cup yellow onion, diced
• 0.5 tablespoon ginger, grated
• 0.5 tablespoon garlic, minced
• 1 cup tomato puree (canned, or 2 blended tomatoes)
• 0.5 cup water
• 1 cup full-fat unsweetened coconut milk
• 1 teaspoon garam masala
• 1 tablespoon lime juice
• 1 teaspoon ground cumin
• 0.5 teaspoon ground turmeric
• 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder (or paprika)
• 1 teaspoon coriander powder
• 1 teaspoon salt
• Cilantro, to garnish

For the air-fried cod:
• 1.25 pounds cod fillets, cut into 4 portions
• 1 tablespoon olive oil
• 0.5 teaspoon garam masala
• 0.5 teaspoon ground turmeric
• 0.5 teaspoon salt
• 0.25 teaspoon black pepper

For serving:
• 3 cups cooked basmati rice
• Lime wedges

STEPS
1. Set Instant Pot to Sauté and heat. Add ghee, then cumin seeds; sizzle 30 seconds.
2. Add onion, ginger, and garlic. Sauté about 3 minutes until onions are golden.
3. Add tomato puree and all ground spices. Sauté 2-3 minutes.
4. Add water and stir, scraping the bottom. Cancel Sauté, seal the lid, and set the vent to sealing.
5. Pressure cook on high for 4 minutes, then quick-release.
6. Stir in coconut milk and garam masala; simmer on Sauté 4-5 minutes until the sauce thickens.
7. Stir in lime juice and season to taste. Keep warm on the Keep Warm setting.
8. While the sauce simmers, pat the cod dry and rub with olive oil, garam masala, turmeric, salt, and pepper.
9. Preheat the air fryer to 400°F. Air-fry the cod for 8-10 minutes, until it flakes easily and reaches 145°F internal temperature. No need to flip.
10. Spoon rice into bowls, ladle coconut curry sauce over the rice, and top each with a piece of air-fried cod. Garnish with cilantro and serve with lime wedges.

NOTES
Cod is delicate, so cook it separately and place it on top rather than simmering it in the curry, which would break it apart. Any firm white fish (haddock, halibut, pollock) works well. If you don't have an air fryer, roast the cod at 425°F for 12-15 minutes or pan-sear 3-4 minutes per side. Stir a handful of baby spinach into the sauce at the end for greens. If your pot scorches easily, add the tomato puree after the water step and don't stir before sealing.
